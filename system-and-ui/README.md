Use [PACKS.json](PACKS.json) for the complete imported geometry snapshots and common symbol names. Use [DIRECT-PACKS.json](DIRECT-PACKS.json) for selected original files from Fluent UI, Material Symbols, Phosphor and Tabler. Those packs organize individual styles in separate directories. Heroicons and Iconoir retain their upstream style suffixes. Alternate names from the snapshot use `aliases.json`, and legacy SVGs have their own directories.

Each pack includes its upstream license and source information. The license and applicable attribution travel with the files. A design pack can include stylized references to products and brands. Refer to the corresponding official asset and brand terms for trademark use.

SVGs with `currentColor` can inherit a surrounding color when inlined in your app. Some filled or multicolor symbols have fixed colors. Pick a style and size that works with the rest of your interface.
