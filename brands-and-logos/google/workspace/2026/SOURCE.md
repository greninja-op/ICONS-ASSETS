# Google Workspace 2026

The current Workspace app icon artwork was sourced from the May 2026 [TheSVG import](https://github.com/glincker/thesvg/pull/401), an exact checked-in SVG distribution derived from Google’s own gstatic product icon URLs. See the [Google announcement](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/05/introducing-fresh-visual-identity-for-Google-Workspace-app-icons.html) and [Google Workspace branding page](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/getting-started/brand-your-internal-communications-with-google-workspace).

The artwork remains Google product branding and follows applicable [Google brand guidance](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/).
