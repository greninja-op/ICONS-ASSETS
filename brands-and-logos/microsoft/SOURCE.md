# Microsoft product marks

SVGs are from the [Microsoft Cloud Product Icons collection](https://github.com/DamoBird365/microsoft-cloud-icons/tree/771f2822c3aee260183bef8e9e857f59a02ccbf4), a curated community mirror of product artwork. Product subfolders follow the source collection. The artwork remains Microsoft’s. Follow the [Microsoft trademark and brand guidance](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/intellectualproperty/trademarks) and the [Microsoft 365 trademark guidelines](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/mscle/documents/presentations/FY26_Microsoft_365_trademark_guidelines.pdf).

Microsoft announced a [Microsoft 365 icon refresh](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365insiderblog/new-microsoft-365-icons-for-the-ai-era/4458674) in 2025. Source-pack recency varies by product.
